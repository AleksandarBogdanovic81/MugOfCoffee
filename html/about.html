who we are

In just two years, Mug of Coffee has become a recognizable part of the neighborhood and a favorite destination for both locals and visitors. Built around the idea of slowing down and enjoying the moment, our café offers a welcoming atmosphere where guests can relax, connect and enjoy carefully crafted coffee in comfort.

We wanted to create more than just a place to grab a drink. Mug of Coffee is designed to feel familiar and inviting — a space where you can unwind with friends, enjoy a quiet break or simply make yourself comfortable as if you were in your own living room. At the same time, it provides the perfect setting for those who want to open a laptop, focus on a project or spend a productive afternoon in a relaxed environment.

As a neighborhood café, we value community, friendly conversations, and a sense of belonging. That is why everyone is welcome here, including four-legged companions. Whether you come for the coffee, the atmosphere, the company or a productive work session, our goal is to make every visit feel comfortable, enjoyable and worth returning for.

coffee origin & quality

Every cup at Mug of Coffee begins with carefully selected coffee beans sourced from the fertile slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania. Grown at high altitudes and harvested with great care, these beans develop a rich aroma, balanced body and distinctive character that make East African coffee celebrated around the world.

The plantations where our coffee is grown are cultivated by families from the Chaga community, whose connection to the land and coffee production has been passed down through generations. Their knowledge, dedication and respect for tradition play an essential role in preserving the exceptional quality of every harvest.

To complement the character of our coffee, we use premium fresh milk supplied by local family farms located on the outskirts of Stara Planina in Serbia. Raised in a clean natural environment and produced with the same commitment to quality that defines our coffee, this milk brings a smooth and creamy texture to every latte and macchiato we serve.

By combining outstanding Tanzanian coffee with carefully sourced local ingredients, we aim to create a coffee experience that reflects authenticity, craftsmanship and respect for the people behind every cup.
